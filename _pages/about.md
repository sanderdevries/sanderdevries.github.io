I am an economist working on **intergenerational mobility, family economics, and the economics of education**. I use large-scale administrative data from the Netherlands and Australia, combined with novel quasi-experimental designs and machine learning approaches, to study how family background and public policy shape children's opportunities.

I obtained my PhD in Economics from the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam in April 2026, under the supervision of [Nadine Ketel](https://sites.google.com/site/nadineketel/) and [Maarten Lindeboom](https://research.vu.nl/en/persons/maarten-lindeboom). Two solo-authored chapters of my dissertation received **wide national media attention** in the Netherlands, including coverage in *NRC*, *de Volkskrant*, *Het Financieele Dagblad*, *De Telegraaf*, *NPO Radio 1*, and *NOS Stories*. My study of performance-based dismissal policies in Dutch higher education **led to questions in the Dutch parliament**, and a university abolished its policy with direct reference to the study. During my PhD, I spent two months as a visiting researcher at the Centre for Economic Performance at the London School of Economics.

I am now a **Postdoctoral Research Fellow** at the [University of Technology Sydney](https://www.uts.edu.au/), where I work with Peter Siminski, Nathan Deutscher, Roger Wilkins, and Bashkar Mazumder on the ARC Discovery Project *Inequality and Intergenerational Mobility: Measuring What Matters and Why*. I am also a Fellow of the [Tax and Transfer Policy Institute](https://crawford.anu.edu.au/ttpi) at the Australian National University.

See my [research page](https://sanderdevries.github.io/research/) for all my research and media coverage.
